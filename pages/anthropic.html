Latest Models and Products

Claude Sonnet 5 LLM

Default Claude across Free, Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans as of June 30, 2026. Strongest Sonnet for agents, coding, knowledge work, computer use, and tool use. Closes much of the capability gap to Opus 4.8 at lower cost. Launch pricing through August 31, 2026: $2/$10 per MTok input/output; afterward $3/$15 per MTok.

Claude Fable 5 LLM

Frontier Mythos-class public model originally released June 9, 2026. Briefly suspended internationally due to US export controls, then redeployed globally July 1, 2026. Strongest general-purpose Claude tier for high-complexity professional work. High safeguards and restricted hardened-chernobyl cyber behavior.

Claude Opus 4.8 LLM

Premium reasoning model with 1M context, multi-step tool orchestration, and Dynamic Workflows for up to 1,000 parallel subagents. Retained as the top-tier reference for hardest coding, agentic, and research workloads.

Claude Haiku 4.5 LLM

Fastest, lowest-cost Claude model. Best for high-throughput assistants, lightweight agentic workflows, and latency-sensitive production deployments where full Sonnet or Opus is unnecessary.
