Latest Models and Products

Mistral Medium 3.5 LLM

Current production flagship powering Mistral’s Vibe remote agents and general API workloads. Latest medium-tier model released in 2026.

Mistral Small 4 LLM

Released March 16, 2026. Apache 2.0. 119B total parameters with 8B active including embeddings/output layers, 256K context, configurable reasoning effort, and native text+image input. Unifies Magistral-style reasoning, Pixtral-style multimodal, and Devstral-style agentic/coding abilities in one model.

Mistral OCR 4 LLM

Document intelligence model announced in 2026 for state-of-the-art OCR, structured extraction, and document understanding.

Voxtral TTS VOICE

Mistral’s text-to-speech model released in 2026, positioned for conversational and assistant voice products.

Ministral 3 family / Codestral LLM

Small dense models for edge deployment and code-specialized variants for developers. Still part of the lineup, though Small 4 and Medium 3.5 are the primary current tracks.

Recent Activity and Context

Mistral has shifted toward a flatter, more productized lineup in 2026: Vibe as the agent product, Medium 3.5 for general production, Small 4 as the open unified model, OCR 4 for document workflows, and Voxtral TTS for voice. The company remains committed to open-weight releases under Apache 2.0 and to NIM/NVIDIA distribution via the Nemotron Coalition.
