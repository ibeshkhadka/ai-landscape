Latest Models and Products

GPT-5.6 Sol LLM

Current flagship released July 9, 2026. State-of-the-art coding, knowledge work, cybersecurity, and science. New state of the art on Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index and Terminal-Bench 2.1. Supports an ultra mode for parallel multi-agent work. Stronger computer use and design judgment. Priced higher than Terra/Luna; introduced at $2/$10 per MTok input/output through August 31, 2026, then $3/$15 per MTok.

GPT-5.6 Terra LLM

Balanced family member for everyday work. Outperforms Claude Fable 5 at roughly 1/16th the estimated cost on broad intelligence tasks with materially lower latency and output-token usage.

GPT-5.6 Luna LLM

Most cost-efficient GPT-5.6 variant for high-throughput production workloads. Strong capability at substantially lower compute cost than earlier-generation premium models.

GPT-Realtime-2 VOICE

Low-latency voice model for realtime conversation and voice-first agent interfaces.

Recent Activity and Context

OpenAI’s current flagship is GPT-5.6, organized as a family rather than one model: Sol for frontier work, Terra for balanced daily use, and Luna for cost-efficient throughput. OpenAI retired GPT-4o and older ChatGPT-era models in February 2026, then added GPT-5.4 mini/Thinking in March, and GPT-5.5 Instant in May. GPT-5.6 is the sharpest break from earlier naming because it introduces sol/terra/luna tiers instead of only instant/thinking/pro splits.
